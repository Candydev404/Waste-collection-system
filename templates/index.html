Household and campus waste collection

Request a waste collection in under a minute.

Tell us who you are, where the waste is, what type it is and when you would like it collected. Requests are recorded centrally so the collection team can organise pickups by area and date.

Request Collection How it works
A request needs four details
  • Your full name
  • Your area or location
  • Preferred collection date
  • Type of waste
You receive a unique reference number when the request is saved.

How it works

Three steps from request to collection.

1

Submit a request

Complete the request form with your name, area, preferred date and waste type.

2

Request is organised

Administrators review new requests and group them by area and collection date.

3

Collection is recorded

Each request moves from Pending to Scheduled and finally Collected.

Service information

  • The date you choose is a preferred date. Past dates are not accepted.
  • Keep your reference number. It identifies your request when you contact the collection team.
  • Requests from the same area are grouped together so collections can be planned efficiently.

About this system

This is an academic prototype developed by Group 26 for NS-CSC 229 Integrated Design Project II at Nasarawa State University, Keffi.

Records shown during demonstrations are synthetic and do not represent real residents or real collections.
